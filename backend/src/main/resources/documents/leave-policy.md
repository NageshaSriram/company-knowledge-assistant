# CedarBridge Leave Policy

This is a fictional policy for a software learning project.

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Applies to: full-time employees based in India.

## Annual leave

Eligible employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave requires approval from the employee's manager.
Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days.

## Sick leave

Eligible employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.
Employees should notify their manager when taking sick leave.
