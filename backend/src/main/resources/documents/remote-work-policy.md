# CedarBridge Remote Work Policy

This is a fictional policy for a software learning project.

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Applies to: full-time employees based in India.

## Remote-work schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
Remote-work days require approval from the employee's manager.
Employees must record approved remote-work days in the team calendar.

## Equipment

The company provides eligible employees with a laptop for work.
Employees must report a lost or damaged company laptop to the IT service desk.
